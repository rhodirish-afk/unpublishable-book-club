Company

About the club

The Unpublishable Book Club Ltd is a private limited company registered in the United Kingdom, with the usual directors, filings, and Companies House obligations. Rory Hanrahan is founding director and owner; additional board appointments are planned as the club grows.

We exist for authors who cannot get a fair hearing because their ideas are too sharp for the current climate — historians, journalists, and thinkers working on forgotten wars, cultural critique, and other subjects polite lists prefer to skip.

The goal is simple and unfashionable: a durable space for free speech. Readers get fresh, unfiltered work. Authors get editing, design, distribution help, and a community that markets them as seriously as their books.

Who it is for

UK (and beyond) free-speech advocates, history enthusiasts, and cultural sceptics — the sort of readers who already orbit outlets like The Spectator, UnHerd, The Telegraph, and Spiked. We are a complement to those worlds, not a bland rival book club.

Tone

Sharp. Literate. Contrarian when the facts warrant it. We will not libel anyone, and we will not pretend controversy is always wisdom — but we refuse the soft censorship that pretends difficult books do not exist.
