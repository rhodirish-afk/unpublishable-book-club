Authors

We spotlight writers of the unpublishables — not for lack of craft, but because they refuse the approved story. Early names from our launch pitch, framed carefully and without inventing claims about third parties:

History · Journalism

Kevin Myers

Veteran journalist and historian. Named in our pitch for Troubles-era history that has remained unpublished in the form the club believes readers deserve — the sort of manuscript mainstream houses often sidestep. Profile and title details will be confirmed with the author before any book launch.

Biography · Security

Dr William Matchett

Associated in our pitch with Concrete & Steel and a biography of Frank Murray — serious, contested ground that fits the club’s brief: rigorous work on subjects many publishers treat as reputationally radioactive. Publication pathway via the club to be announced with the author.

More coming

Historians, journalists, and essayists who have been told “brilliant — but not for us.” If that is your manuscript, we want to see it.
