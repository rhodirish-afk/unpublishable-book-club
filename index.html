The pitch, briefly

A push against soft censorship

Mainstream lists favour safe taste. Historians, journalists, and thinkers with edgy or heterodox ideas — forgotten wars, cultural critique, inconvenient biography — often find the door closed. We open another one.

We sell the authors as much as the books: profiles, events, and shared marketing that turn solo writers into a movement. Readers get unfiltered work; authors get editing, design, tech help, and a community that actually wants the argument.
